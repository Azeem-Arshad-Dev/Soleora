About Soleora

Soleora is a showroom for the shoes most people own, or wonder about. Each of the eighteen styles here gets a short, plain-spoken article: where it came from, the features that give it away, what it is made of, when to wear it, how it should fit and how to look after it.

Every shoe in the showroom is a 3D model you can turn in your hands. Where a suitable openly licensed model exists we use it, credited below; the rest are built in code, so think of those as a cobbler's sketch rather than a product photo.

How we write

We avoid prices, brand endorsements and claims we can't support. Where history is uncertain, we say so. If you spot something wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.
